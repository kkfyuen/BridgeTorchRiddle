Puzzle Rules & Constraints

  • Torch Required: No one can cross the bridge without carrying the single torch.
  • Max 2 Walkers: At most 2 people can cross at the exact same time.
  • Slower Pace Rule: When 2 people cross together, they walk at the slower person's speed.
  • Time Limit: The torch only lasts 15 minutes total!
  • The Trick: Group the slowest walkers together to save overall time!
